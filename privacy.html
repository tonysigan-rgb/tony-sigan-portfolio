Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE 5 NOVEMBER 2026

pluffy minis is a website for watching original short dramas, run by Tony Sigan Anak Lapok in Kuching, Sarawak, Malaysia ("we", "us"). It is available at tonysigan-rgb.github.io/tony-sigan-portfolio/pluffyminis.html. This policy explains what information the site uses and why.

Short version: we do not have accounts, a database or a server of our own. We don't collect, sell or share your personal information. If you choose to sign in with Google, we only check whether you are subscribed to our YouTube channel, then forget it.

1. Signing in with Google (optional)

New episodes are available early to subscribers of our YouTube channel, @itsmetslFilms. If you tap "Subscribe & watch now" and continue with Google, Google asks you to allow this site to view and manage your YouTube account (the youtube.force-ssl permission). We use it for exactly two things:

  • Check: we ask YouTube whether your account is already subscribed to @itsmetslFilms.
  • Subscribe: only if you are not subscribed yet, we subscribe your account to @itsmetslFilms, because that is what you asked for by tapping the button. Nothing else is ever changed. You can unsubscribe on YouTube at any time.
  • What we don't access or change: we don't read your name, email address, watch history, playlists or other subscriptions, and we never post, comment, like, upload or delete anything.
  • Where it happens: everything runs in your own browser. The access token Google gives your browser is never sent to us or to any server we run, and it is revoked straight after.
  • What we keep: nothing on our side. Your browser saves a small note that early episodes are unlocked (in local storage) for 7 days. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.
  • Revoking access: you can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

pluffy minis' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't use this information for advertising, we don't sell it, we don't transfer it to anyone, and no person reads it.

2. Videos from YouTube

Episodes and teasers are played through YouTube's embedded player. When you watch, YouTube (Google) may collect information under its own policies. See the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Things saved in your browser

The site saves a few small settings in your browser's local storage, such as whether auto-play of the next episode is on, and the 7-day unlock note above. These stay on your device and are never sent to us.

4. Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep technical logs such as IP addresses for security, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We don't use analytics or advertising trackers.

5. Children

pluffy minis is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
